About Us

A student team, run like a real IT department

Nexivra is the technical services team of NewBridge Graduate Institute — staffed by students pursuing Systems & Network Support, Cloud Administration & Cybersecurity, Computer Programming & Web Development, and Web AI & Software Programming.

Each technician trains on real repair, networking and cloud-support work as part of their coursework, and every engagement is delivered with structured diagnostics, transparent pricing and clear communication.

A number of team members have completed Level 1 coursework and are now progressing into Level 2, with several having switched fields entirely to broaden the team's technical range. IT Heroes operates as a supervised, work-ready unit — every repair is reviewed under institute oversight.
